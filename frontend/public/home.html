Margarita, an on-chain gacha game

Pay 0.001 tBNB on BNB Chain to open a barrel. A verifiable random roll picks one of four tiers, and 3 Blanco, 3 Reposado or 7 Añejo age into one of the next. Live on the testnet now.

How the game works

  1. Pull a barrel for 0.001 tBNB

    Sign one transaction. A few blocks later the random roll lands and the tier is in your wallet as an ERC-1155 token.

  2. Age 3 tokens into 1

    Three Reposado become one Añejo after five minutes. Seven Añejo make an Extra Añejo.

  3. Check every roll from 0 to 9 999

    Each reveal lists its roll and the rule that turned it into a tier, with its transaction on BscScan.

One barrel, 100 bottles

Extra Añejo tokens will redeem for real bottles from one barrel, capped at 100. Redeeming opens once delivery is set up.

Questions

About money, randomness and the bottles.

Does the game cost real money?

No. Margarita runs on BNB Chain testnet, where BNB comes free from a faucet and has no value.

What if the random roll never comes back?

After an hour the owner can cancel the pull. Your tBNB is credited, and you claim it in My cellar.

Why does aging cost 3 tokens?

It trades up: 3 Blanco for 1 Reposado, 3 Reposado for 1 Añejo, 7 Añejo for 1 Extra Añejo.

When does the game open redeeming?

Not yet. Redeeming opens once delivery is set up. Until then an Extra Añejo stays in your wallet.

Has the game contract been audited?

Not by an outside firm. It is a testnet demo, and the contract is unaudited.
